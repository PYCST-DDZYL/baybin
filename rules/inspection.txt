Food you could pick up in or on any container means item=food_in_container and food=present, including ceramic bowls, mugs and plates, glass jars, metal cans, aluminum trays and foil. Soup or porridge with visible solid food counts. The container's material never overrides visible food. An empty stained pizza box is pizza_box_greasy. Non-container objects can use food=none.
A folded milk or juice carton is carton, not paperboard_box. Use printed milk/juice text or a clear gable-top carton identity. A cleaner or oil label must not become an ordinary drink bottle.
material: copy a clearly readable plastic recycling number 1 through 7, or compostable for clearly readable COMPOSTABLE/BIODEGRADABLE/PLA/MADE FROM CORN text. Those words override a number if both are visible. Unreadable, hidden, or absent marking is unknown. Never infer a number from shape or transparency.
empty: yes only when the opening/interior visibly proves it empty. A closed aerosol can cannot be proved completely empty from a photo; use unknown. Non-container objects can use unknown.
paper: for cardboard_box, paperboard_box and pizza_box_greasy, clean means visible inner and outer surfaces have no food stains or grease; soiled means visible food stains or grease. A closed food box has unknown food and paper, even if its outside looks clean. Do not turn a closed pizza box into recyclable paperboard. For other paper food containers, uncoated requires a visible open interior without plastic/foil coating; otherwise coated or unknown. Non-paper objects use unknown.
For carton/aseptic_carton only, paper=gable needs a clear gable top AND readable fresh/refrigerated milk or juice packaging. paper=aseptic needs readable aseptic/UHT/long-life/shelf-stable markings or visible foil-layer construction. Refrigerate AFTER OPENING does not establish a fresh carton. Shape, a pour spout, or the word milk alone is insufficient; use paper=unknown when the type cannot be confirmed. The Tetra Pak company name alone does not prove a carton is aseptic.
label: milk, juice, cleaner, oil, other, or unknown. Use other for a clearly identified non-cleaner/non-oil drink label, e.g. water or soda. A hidden or unreadable label is unknown.
